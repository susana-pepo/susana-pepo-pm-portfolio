03 — SELECTED WORK (Case studies)
=====================================

Panel title: Four projects that shaped how I work.


--- Case 1 ---
Title: Song Discovery
Subtitle: From a buried catalogue to the number one way users find songs.
Scope: Product Owner · Skoove · with design, engineering & QA
Headline metric: #1 discovery method
Tags: Product Discovery, A/B Testing, User Research, Funnel Optimization

Problem: Skoove had a large song catalogue, but discovery ran entirely through curated collections. That worked for new users and frustrated returning learners who knew exactly what they wanted to play.

What I did: Contributed across discovery, scoping, and post-launch analysis for both the Song Search MVP and the Favorites feature. Coordinated QA across platforms and tracked results in Mixpanel. For Favorites I designed unmoderated usability research in Maze, led KPI tracking, and wrote the post-experiment report.

Outcome: Search became the number one discovery method in the Songs tab, ahead of suggested songs. Favorites hit its primary KPI: 30% of premium users who visited Songs saved at least one. A search-paywall A/B test fed a strategic decision on free-trial access.


--- Case 2 ---
Title: Paywall & Onboarding Experiments
Subtitle: Turning first impressions into paying users.
Scope: Product Owner · Trala & Skoove · subscription experiments
Headline metric: +28.7% revenue / visitor
Tags: Subscription A/B Testing, Onboarding, Conversion, Experiment Design

Problem: The paywall and the first-run experience were doing less than they could. New users arrived with very different goals, and everyone saw the same thing.

What I did: Ran subscription A/B tests on RevenueCat and Mixpanel, owning each from hypothesis and metric design through to read-out and follow-up. Personalized onboarding with location-based content and questionnaire variants on genre and learning goal, so the product adapted to why each person came.

Outcome: Projected revenue per paywall visitor rose 28.7%. The onboarding changes contributed to 14% year-on-year revenue growth.


--- Case 3 ---
Title: The Commercial Behind the Catalogue
Subtitle: The licensing and negotiation work that lets people learn the songs they actually listen to.
Scope: Lead negotiator · Skoove & Trala · publishers & rights organizations
Headline metric: 14.7% cost ratio, from 18%
Tags: Commercial Negotiation, Licensing, Rights Strategy, Stakeholder Management

Problem: Every popular song is wrapped in a web of publishers, performance rights organizations, and territory rules. Putting that music inside a learning app, at a cost that works, is a negotiation before it is a feature.

What I did: Led the negotiation that unlocked the Disney Music Group catalogue, our largest Disney piano collection against the competition. Worked publisher by publisher on multi-year terms, royalty mechanics, rights clearance, and dispute resolution, as the direct interlocutor for the company. Trained in negotiation by the CEO and CPO.

Outcome: A contemporary, high-demand catalogue users want, with the licensing cost ratio improved from 18% to 14.7% of revenue.


--- Case 4 ---
Title: Content Operations at Scale
Subtitle: Growing the catalogue 160% while keeping quality and licensing coverage intact.
Scope: Content Manager → Product Owner · Skoove & Trala · cross-functional
Headline metric: +160% catalogue growth
Tags: Content Ops, Process Design, Localization, Cross-functional Delivery

Problem: Running content for an app at this size means shipping constantly, new songs, seasonal collections, and localized versions, without letting quality slip. The workflows were manual and fragmented.

What I did: Worked on three levels at once. Rebuilt the internal content tooling to reduce engineer dependency. Ran bulk localization across 7+ languages with coordinated QA, including market pushes into Japan and Korea. Built the licensing foundation, partner pages, a content-tactics matrix, and rights-ownership workflows, now used across Engineering, Content, and Marketing.

Outcome: The catalogue grew 160% with licensing coverage and quality held intact. End-to-end process time down 70%. Quarterly releases shipping up to 260+ songs at a time, on schedule, with content decisions tied to real usage data.
